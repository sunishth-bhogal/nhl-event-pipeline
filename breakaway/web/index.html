        How the win probability works

        After every play, a logistic-regression model looks at the score, the time left, power plays, a pulled goalie and each team's pregame rating, and estimates who wins. Ratings only use games played before that night, and the model is tested on a full season it never saw.

        Play-by-play from the NHL's public web API. Breakaway is a personal project, not affiliated with the NHL.
